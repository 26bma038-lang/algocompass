How it works

  1. Install and answer one question

    New to DSA? You start on step 1 of the Beginner Path. Already practising? Add your Codeforces handle.

  2. Do the one thing on Today

    Today shows a single next action: watch a lesson, or open a problem on LeetCode or Codeforces.

  3. It ticks itself

    Solve it on the site. At the next update your goals, sheets and streak catch up on their own.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. AlgoCompass is free to download and use. There are no ads and nothing to sign up for.

Where does my data go?

It stays on your PC. Your progress lives in a local database in your Windows user folder. The app only reads your public Codeforces and LeetCode profiles, and it has no analytics or telemetry.

Do I need accounts?

Not to start learning. Beginners can begin the Beginner Path without any account. To track your practice, enter your Codeforces handle and, optionally, your LeetCode username. You never type a password.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything is cached on your PC, so your progress, sheets and lessons open without internet and update when you're back online. Videos need a connection to play.

Is there a Mac version?

Not yet. AlgoCompass runs on Windows 10 and 11.

Will updating lose my progress?

No. Download the new installer and run it. Your solves, sheets and settings stay where they are.
